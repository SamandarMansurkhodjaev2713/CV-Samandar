02 / 05

Context

What it is

A telemetry dashboard prototype for monitoring a biogas station: realtime flow metrics, threshold alarms and reporting.

The problem it solves

An industrial station needs live control of flow, alarms and investor reporting — instead of manual spreadsheets and late summaries.

03 / 05

System

Technical stack

ReactRechartsTypeScriptVite

Architecture

A data layer normalizes telemetry, the dashboard renders metrics and trends with Recharts, threshold logic raises alarms, and a reporting layer assembles investor summaries.

Why this architecture

Industrial monitoring needs to stay readable under load: clear thresholds, trends and alarms matter more than decorative charts — so the focus is on data and a legible presentation.

04 / 05

Evidence

Strengths

An industrial context, realtime telemetry, threshold alarms and investor-oriented reporting in a clean, typed frontend.

What it proves

The ability to turn raw telemetry into a legible operational and investor dashboard: data, thresholds, trends and reports.

Quality · QA

How quality was engineered

Strict typing (TypeScript) and separation of threshold logic from rendering: alarms are testable apart from the charts, so a trigger is deterministic rather than eyeballed.

05 / 05

Boundaries

!
Honest boundaries

The status is prototype, not an industrial system: it was exercised on test and simulated data, not on a certified plant loop. The product does not replace an industrial safety system and carries no SLA. Long-term history retention, fault tolerance and SCADA integration are the next stage, not something already done.

Context

What it is

A private on-prem collaboration platform for teams: projects, tasks, chat, files, search, notifications, roles and audit logs — a workspace without moving internal data into an external SaaS.

The problem it solves

Some teams need modern collaboration tools but cannot or will not send internal data to third-party services. The platform gives a controlled environment where communication, files and project management stay inside the organization's own infrastructure.

Technical stack

Architecture

Split by domains: identity, workspaces, projects, tasks, messaging, files, search, notifications, audit, a realtime gateway and background workers. File storage is separated from the database, and access control runs through a dedicated role model and workspace boundaries.

Why this architecture

Enterprise collaboration turns chaotic when everything lives in one module. Domain separation improves evolution, security, auditability, testing and on-prem deployment.
